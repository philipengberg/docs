## Your data, on your terms

The Min Strøm Consumer API gives **Min Strøm Plus** subscribers read-only access to their own addresses, electricity consumption, solar export, calculated costs, and prices.

Create an API key in the iOS app, then use it in your own integration. Your key identifies your account.

## What you can read

- Address summaries and the capabilities available for each address.
- Electricity consumption and solar-export series.
- Calculated consumption costs and solar-export revenue.
- Confirmed historical prices and available hourly forecast prices.

Available data varies by address. A successful response may contain fewer readings than the requested period: missing data is not zero.
