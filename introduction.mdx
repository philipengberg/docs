## Your data, on your terms

The Min Strøm Consumer API gives **Min Strøm Plus** subscribers read-only access to their own addresses, electricity consumption, solar export, calculated costs, and prices.

Create an API key in the iOS app, then use it in your own integration. You do not need to supply your user ID: your key identifies your account, and each request is restricted to your addresses.

## What you can read

- Address summaries and the capabilities available for each address.
- Electricity consumption and solar-export series.
- Calculated consumption costs and solar-export revenue.
- Confirmed historical prices and available hourly forecast prices.

Availability depends on the region and the meters or devices configured for each address. A successful response may contain fewer readings than the requested period: missing data is not zero.

The Consumer API does not modify your account, control charging, or expose other users' data.
